EVERYDAY FINDS - SIMPLE STOREFRONT

1. Open index.html to preview locally.
2. Edit index.html: replace 'Everyday Finds' with your business name.
3. Edit the products array to change product names, prices, descriptions, and images.
4. Set SELLER_EMAIL to your real email address if you want the email order button to work.
5. Publish by dragging this whole folder (or the extracted ZIP contents) to a static hosting service such as Netlify Drop.

IMPORTANT: This is a storefront/catalog with a working browser cart and copy-order flow. It does NOT accept online payments, automatically send orders, or track inventory. No customer information is collected. Confirm pricing and product details before publishing. The puzzle photo shows multiple puzzle designs and should eventually be replaced with separate product photos.
